How I work

I'm a QA engineer and test automation architect with 20+ years of building quality systems — for financial-grade platforms at E*TRADE and TransUnion, mobile at eBay and Intel Security, and a string of startups where I was the first QA hire and quality strategy started with me. Now I'm focused on what AI changes about that job: replacing manual test cases and brittle regression cycles with agents that explore UIs, exercise APIs, and reason about failures instead of just detecting them.

Everything on this site was designed, built, and shipped by me — several of these tools run in my client engagements today. When we work together, the person you talk to is the person doing the work. Engagements usually start with a conversation about where quality is hurting, and a scan of the codebase in question.
